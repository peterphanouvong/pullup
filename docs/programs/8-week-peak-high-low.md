# 8-Week Peak — high/low revision (Peter's instance)

Applied to Peter's own instance of the 8-Week Peak by
`supabase/migrations/20261021120000_high_low_peak.sql`. The template in
`docs/programs/8-week-peak.md` is unchanged; this doc records how the instance
differs from it.

**Replaces** the Monday/Tuesday/Thursday/Friday split from 26 October onward.
The week of 19 Oct finishes as planned: it's the deload, the cheapest possible
moment to change structure.

**The change in one line:** fatigue gets concentrated into three hard days
instead of smeared across six, and volleyball is counted as training rather
than ignored by it.

## The week

| Day | Load | Session |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mon | **High** | Sprints, cleans, jumps, front squat |
| Tue | Low | Upper strength — "armour" |
| Wed | **High** | Heavy lower (midday) + court (night) |
| Thu | Low | Upper, lighter — pull and shoulder health |
| Fri | Low | Primer — leave feeling better than you arrived |
| Sat | **High** | Court (morning) |
| Sun | Off | Rest |

No two high days are adjacent: Mon→Wed is 48 h, Wed→Sat 72 h, Sat→Mon 48 h.

**The big change is Wednesday.** Heavy squats go on the same day as court,
not the day after. The cost of a hard day is paid once, and Thursday becomes
a genuine recovery day instead of a compromised training day.

**If you also play Monday**, lift first, play at night, and cut the depth
jumps.

**The trade-off:** Wednesday is now a genuinely big day and court quality that
night may dip for a week or two. If after two weeks Wednesday court is
consistently poor, lift in the evening after court instead. If Monday feels
heavy rather than fast, cut Wednesday to cleans and squats only.

Saturday evening is recovery, not celebration: protein and carbs within an

## What stays from the template

Training maxes at 90% of estimated 1RM, recalculated 26 Oct. Bar speed
governs cleans. Durability work (calf raises and Copenhagen planks on
Wednesday, pogo hops on Monday) is non-negotiable in the fortnight before the
tournament. Full warm-up before Saturday court. Sleep 7–9 hours, protein
1.6–2.0 g/kg. Comp-day fuelling as in the template.
