Plan a program

Answer a few questions about your days, your equipment and what you're training for, and the coach writes a full block for you, down to the sets. Swap an exercise, run the short version of a day when you're pressed for time, or move the week along if you fall behind.

Finish, and it counts

Every session you finish becomes a card: time under the bar, volume, sets, your face on it, and how long until the day you're training for. Share it, or let it sit in the log where it adds up.

Train with friends

Add your mates to a crew. Every finished session shows up for them, with your face on it, so a skipped Tuesday doesn't go unnoticed and a good week does.

See it add up

Every set shows what you did last time, so progress is a number you can beat rather than a feeling. Finished sessions collect on your profile, and a good month looks like one.
